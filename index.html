Why We Exist

Founded for one rider. Run for thousands.

Straightway began with a single need inside our own family: a grandmother who deserved more than a ride — she deserved an escort. Someone trained, trusted, and patient enough to walk her from her door, through the lobby, to the chair, and home again. We built that service because it didn't exist. Then we built a company around it.

Today we serve managed-care members across New Jersey as a contracted transportation provider, with a focus on the riders who need us most: recurring dialysis patients on standing schedules, ambulatory riders, and door-to-door assist members who need a driver who gets out of the vehicle.

“The mission is simple to say and hard to do: treat every rider like she is your own grandmother — and be exactly on time.”

Straightway Founding Principle

Standards & Credentialing

No clearance, no wheel.

We run Straightway like a unit. Every driver is credentialed before dispatch and re-verified continuously — and our systems enforce it automatically. A driver who falls out of compliance cannot log in, cannot be dispatched, and cannot be paid.

Credentialed Drivers

Background checks, fingerprinting, drug screening, and jurisdiction-specific clearance for every driver in every market — synced to dispatch twice daily.

Verified Identity

Document-verified driver and rider identity in every chapter, using each jurisdiction's national standard.

Privacy by Design

Rider health information is separated by jurisdiction, encrypted in backup, and never shared across markets. HIPAA-clean communications are standard procedure.

Audited Daily

Every trip we run is reconciled against source records every day — mileage, level of service, and payment — so nothing is missed and nothing is misreported.

The Platform

A provider, in a box.

We didn't buy our operations software — we built it, trip by trip, on real routes with real riders. The Straightway Network platform runs our dispatch, and it's being engineered so any transportation provider, in any jurisdiction, can one day run on it.
